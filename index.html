Research & patents

Generative AI, biosignal modelling and fair ML.

  1. [1]
    Oral · Patent filedIEEE EMBC 2026

    AtomSigNet: Biosignal Diffusion with Markovian Reasoning

    A Markovian reasoning engine with conditional diffusion that corrects sensor artifacts, plus a test-time scaling mechanism that adjusts compute on the fly — 91% SOTA accuracy on WESAD/CASE (+14% F1) with 5× fewer labels. The architecture is the subject of a filed US patent.

  2. [2]
    WorkshopAFRL Quantum AI4 · 2026

    MIND-DiT: Multimodal Incremental Neural Diffusion Transformer for EEG-to-Image Synthesis

    Personalised EEG-to-image synthesis with adaptive identity evolution via few-shot incremental learning, a unified neural-visual transformer backbone and a continually evolved classifier that prevents catastrophic forgetting — 15–20% accuracy gain over U-Net baselines.

  3. [3]
    ConferenceMHSRS · 2026

    Data to Decision: Optimizing Cognitive Performance for the Operational Environment

    Translating biosignal-driven cognitive-state models into decision-support tooling for operational environments, building on the AtomSigNet reasoning engine.

  4. [4]
    Peer-reviewedIEEE BSN · 2025

    AFairDNet: Fair Multisensor Emotion Recognition System

    A chain-of-thought loop that scores synthetic fairness with CLIP and entropy metrics and refactors prompts dynamically — beating SOTA baselines by 4% F1 on WESAD using only 1.4% synthetic data.
